When sending messages through OTPIQ, you might wonder if you need a dedicated Sender ID (your brand name) for your communications. The requirements and options vary depending on the messaging channel you are using.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="SMS">
For SMS, registering a Sender ID is **optional**. When registered, you can send messages using your own brand name instead of a generic number.

<Info>
The price for SMS is determined by the carrier and can change over time. Please check the [OTPIQ main website](https://otpiq.com) for the most up-to-date pricing.
</Info>

### Registration requirements

Registering an SMS Sender ID requires a **valid Iraqi company license**. 

The Sender ID can differ from your official company name, but you must provide proof of connection between the brand and the company. Acceptable proof includes:
- Trademark registration
- Having the company name explicitly stated on the brand's website or app

</Tab>

<Tab title="WhatsApp">
By default, all WhatsApp messages are sent under the official OTPIQ account.

### Using the default OTPIQ account

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Benefits" icon="check" color="green">
    - **No setup required**: You can start using it directly
    - **Unlimited messaging**: You don't have to worry about hitting tier limits
  </Card>
  <Card title="Downsides" icon="xmark" color="red">
    - You cannot see user responses to your messages
    - Your brand name is not shown (OTPIQ's name is displayed)
    - You can only send OTP (One-Time Password) messages
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Using your own WhatsApp account

You can use your own WhatsApp account to send messages with your brand name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your business">
    You must have a valid, verified **Meta Business Account** at [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com). 
    
    <Warning>
      This is different from having a verified Facebook page (blue badge). You specifically need a verified Meta Business Account.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your account">
    Once verified, connect your WhatsApp account in the [OTPIQ Dashboard](https://app.otpiq.com/connect-whatsapp-account).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The pricing remains exactly the same whether you use the default OTPIQ account or your own connected WhatsApp account.
</Note>
</Tab>

<Tab title="Telegram">
For Telegram, there is currently **no way** to register a custom brand name or Sender ID.
</Tab>
